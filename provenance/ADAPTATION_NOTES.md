## Checkpoint semantics

Generator records include input/algorithm fingerprints. Compatible saved heavy records may be reused, but every reported test is executed again; no release status is inferred from an inherited success=true field. Initial fresh calculations and a final resumed validation, where applicable, are separately identified in the execution report.
